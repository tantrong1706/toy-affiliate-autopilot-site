Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Overview

Toy Affiliate Autopilot is a desktop application designed to help creators prepare and publish product photo content to their authorized TikTok accounts.

2. Information Processed

When a user connects a TikTok account, the app may process information permitted by the user through TikTok authorization, such as basic profile information and authorization tokens.

3. TikTok Authorization

Access to TikTok is performed using TikTok's official authorization process. The app only requests permissions required for its features.

4. Local Storage

The current desktop version of Toy Affiliate Autopilot runs locally on the user's computer. Application configuration, product data, and authorization credentials may be stored locally on that device.

5. Data Sharing

Toy Affiliate Autopilot does not sell personal information. Information is only transmitted to third-party services when required to perform a user-requested function, such as communicating with TikTok APIs.

6. Content

Product images, captions, and related content may be processed by the app for the purpose of preparing or publishing content requested by the user.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect locally stored credentials and application data. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own computer and accounts.

8. Data Removal

Users may disconnect their TikTok account and remove locally stored application data from their computer.

9. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or data practices change.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the application owner through the contact information provided with the application.
